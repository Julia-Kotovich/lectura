Instructions: Scenario: You're building a notification system. Initially, only email notifications are needed. (1) Design a simple class structure (just class names and key methods). Speaker notes

Debrief focuses on how TDD encourages designing for testability (dependency injection, interfaces), which naturally leads to SOLID compliance. Emphasize: agile doesn't mean no design—it means design emerges from real needs, validated by tests. This synthesizes OO principles with agile practices, addressing the implementation objective by having students actually design and refactor.
