## Suggested Results paragraph

Three independent reviewers rated ten MCSSE-SE-01 lecture decks generated by Lectura on a 1–5 scale for Correctness, Completeness, Clarity, and Ready-to-teach, and answered whether each deck would be usable with students after light editing (Yes / With edits / No). The module-level mean across all numeric criteria was **2.78/5**. Correctness was the strongest dimension (**3.43**), while Ready-to-teach (**2.33**) and Clarity (**2.57**) were the weakest. Across 30 usability judgments, **60%** were *No*, **40%** *With edits*, and **0%** *Yes*. By majority vote, lectures 1–7 and 10 were labeled *No*; only lectures 8–9 reached a majority of *With edits*. Inter-rater disagreement was large (evaluator means 2.05–3.95), but the shared signal is that the decks remain draft-quality rather than classroom-ready.

## Methods note (1–2 sentences)

Each deck was scored independently on four 1–5 criteria (Correctness, Completeness, Clarity, Ready-to-teach) plus a categorical usability item (Yes / With edits / No). Reported lecture means are arithmetic averages over three evaluators; the majority usable label is the majority vote per lecture.
