## 1. BSc Computer Science — Fall 2025

**Степень:** Bachelor of Science (BSc) in Computer Science  
**Объём:** 180 ECTS, 6 семестров, 3 года  
**Действует для:** студентов, поступивших осенью 2025

### Концепция

Фокус на фундаментальных принципах CS (программирование, абстракции, теория вычислений, БД, ОС, сети, безопасность, этика). Следует рекомендациям GI (Германия) и ACM/IEEE CS Curricula 2013. Раннее вовлечение в research, международная среда.

**Координатор:** Prof. Dr. Alexander Omelchenko (`aomelchenko@constructor.university`)

### Модель 4C (180 CP)

| Год | Блок | CP |
|-----|------|-----|
| 1 | **CHOICE** | 45 |
| 2 | **CORE** | 45 |
| 3 | **CAREER** | 45 |
| Все 3 года | **CONSTRUCTOR Track** (methods, skills, humanities) | 45 |

### Year 1 — обязательные CHOICE-модули CS (30 CP)

- Programming in C and C++ (7.5)
- Algorithms and Data Structures (7.5)
- Mathematical Foundations of Computer Science (7.5)
- Digital Systems and Computer Architecture (7.5)

Оставшиеся 15 CP CHOICE — по интересам или для смены major / minor. Без minor во 2-м семестре — Development in JVM Languages (7.5).

### Year 2 — обязательные CORE (30 CP)

- Databases (7.5)
- Software Engineering (7.5)
- Operating Systems (7.5)
- Automata, Computability, and Complexity (7.5)

**Elective CORE (15 CP)** — для minor подменяются:

- Functional Programming (5)
- Legal and Ethical Aspects of Computer Science (2.5)
- Machine Learning (5)
- Academic Skills in Computer Science (2.5)

### Year 3 — CAREER

- **Стажировка** 15 CP (лето после 2-го года; альтернатива — start-up)
- **Specialization** 15 CP (выбор из портфеля модулей)
- **Bachelor Thesis** 12 CP + **Seminar** 3 CP
- Возможность study abroad (обычно 5-й семестр)

**Specialization modules (выбор 15 CP):**

Computer Graphics, Image Processing, Distributed Algorithms, Web Application Development, Computer Networks, Secure and Dependable Systems, Ethical Hacking, Security Monitoring, Linux Kernel Internals, Advanced Operating Systems, Computer Vision, HCI, AI, Robotics, Digital Design, Information Theory, Functional Programming.

**Специализация Cybersecurity:** Secure and Dependable Systems + Ethical Hacking + Security Monitoring + thesis по теме cybersecurity.

### CONSTRUCTOR Track

- **Methods** (20 CP): Elements of Linear Algebra, Elements of Calculus, Probability and Random Processes, Numerical Methods или Discrete Mathematics
- **New Skills** (15 CP): Logic, Causation & Correlation, Argumentation/Data Visualization/Communication, Linear Model/Matrices или Complex Problem Solving, Agency/Leadership или Community Impact Project
- **German / Humanities** (10 CP)

### Требования к выпуску (без minor)

- 180 CP всего
- 20 CP Methods (математика)
- **97.5 CP** CS-модули
- 15 CP Bachelor thesis + seminar
- 15 CP internship
- 25 CP Constructor Track (general part)

**С minor:** 75 CP CS + 30 CP minor.

### Program Learning Outcomes (16 пунктов)

Профессиональная работа в CS, фундаментальные концепции, аналитическое мышление, математика, программирование, алгоритмы, БД, ОС, сети, безопасность, этика, коммуникация, teamwork, lifelong learning и др.

### CS-модули в handbook (Chapter 7)

Programming in C/C++, Algorithms & DS, Mathematical Foundations, Digital Systems, Development in JVM Languages, Databases, Software Engineering, Operating Systems, Machine Learning, Functional Programming, Automata/Computability/Complexity, Legal & Ethical Aspects, Academic Skills, Computer Networks, Secure and Dependable Systems, Security Monitoring, Ethical Hacking, Advanced OS, Linux Kernel, Computer Graphics, Image Processing, Distributed Algorithms, Web App Development, Computer Vision, HCI, AI, Robotics, Internship, Bachelor Thesis & Seminar.

### Поступление (undergraduate)

SAT/ACT (если применимо), transcripts, motivation statement, English TOEFL 90 / IELTS 6.5.

## 2. BSc Computer Science — Fall 2023

**Степень:** BSc in Computer Science  
**Объём:** 180 ECTS, 6 семестров  
**Действует для:** студентов, поступивших осенью 2023 (V1.5, актуально с Jun 2025)

Та же модель **4C** и тот же университет. Структура похожа, но **содержание модулей отличается** от когорты 2025.

### Ключевые отличия от Fall 2025

| Аспект | Fall 2023 | Fall 2025 |
|--------|-----------|-----------|
| CHOICE (30 CP) | C/C++, Algo & DS, **Intro to CS**, **Intro to Robotics** | C/C++, Algo & DS, **Math Foundations**, **Digital Systems** |
| CORE (30 CP) | **Databases and Web Services** (объединённый) | **Databases** (отдельно) |
| Elective CORE для minor | Networks, Legal & Ethical, **Secure & Dependable**, Academic Skills | Functional Programming, Legal & Ethical, **Machine Learning**, Academic Skills |
| CS CP без minor | **90 CP** | **97.5 CP** |
| Модулей в Ch. 7 | 28 | 31+ |
| Специализация Cybersecurity | Да (3 security-модуля + thesis) | Да |

### Year 1 — обязательные CHOICE (30 CP)

- Programming in C and C++ (7.5)
- Algorithms and Data Structures (7.5)
- Introduction to Computer Science (7.5) — абстракции, формальные машины, functional programming
- Introduction to Robotics and Intelligent Systems (7.5)

### Year 2 — обязательные CORE (30 CP)

- Databases and Web Services (7.5)
- Software Engineering (7.5)
- Operating Systems (7.5)
- Automata, Computability, and Complexity (7.5)

**Elective CORE (15 CP):** Computer Networks, Legal & Ethical Aspects, Secure and Dependable Systems, Academic Skills in CS.

### Year 3

Аналогично 2025: стажировка 15 CP, specialization 15 CP, thesis 12 + seminar 3 CP.

**Specialization modules:** Graphics, Image Processing, Distributed Algorithms, Web App Dev, Ethical Hacking, Security Monitoring, Computer Vision, HCI, AI, Robotics, Machine Learning, Digital Design, Information Theory, Parallel and Distributed Computing; для minor — Networks, Legal & Ethical, Secure & Dependable, Academic Skills.

### Требования к выпуску (без minor)

- 180 CP
- 20 CP Methods
- **90 CP** CS-модули
- 15 CP thesis + seminar
- 15 CP internship
- 25 CP Constructor Track

### Minor in CS (для других major)

30 CP: Programming in C/C++, Algorithms & DS, Databases and Web Services, Software Engineering.

## 3. MSc Computer Science and Software Engineering (CSSE) — Fall 2025

**Степень:** Master of Science (MSc) in Computer Science and Software Engineering  
**Объём:** 120 ECTS, 4 семестра, 2 года  
**Действует для:** студентов, поступивших осенью 2025 (V1.3)

### Концепция

Последовательная магистратура: глубокая техническая подготовка + management и leadership. Три технических направления:

1. **Software Engineering** (ядро программы)
2. **Cybersecurity**
3. **Artificial Intelligence**

Плюс breakthrough-модуль **Quantum Informatics**. Формат: flipped classroom, agile team projects, практика на стыке research / industry / start-up.

**Координатор:** Prof. Dr. Jürgen Schönwälder (`jschoenwaelder@constructor.university`)

### Структура 120 ECTS

| Блок | CP |
|------|-----|
| Technical CORE | **45** |
| Management | **15** |
| Leadership / Academic Skills | **15** |
| Startup / Industry / Research orientation | **15** |
| Master Thesis | **30** |

### Семестры

**Семестр 1 (25 CP):**

- Software Construction, Architecture and Engineering (m, 5)
- Quality Engineering (m, 5)
- 1 elective CORE (5)
- Capstone Project I **или** Research Lab I (5)
- Agile Product Development & Design (m, 5)
- Entrepreneurship & Intrapreneurship (2.5) + Communication & Presentation Skills for Executives (2.5)

**Семестр 2 (30 CP):**

- Architectural Strategy (m, 5)
- 2 elective CORE (10)
- Capstone Project II **или** Research Lab II (5)
- Product Innovation & Marketing (m, 5)
- Organizational Behavior (2.5) + Academic Writing Skills / Intercultural Training (2.5)

**Семестр 3 (30 CP):**

- 3 elective CORE (15; один можно заменить на Research Project)
- Capstone III / Internship / Research Project (5–10 CP по треку)
- Transformational Change Management (m, 5)
- Agile Leadership and Strategic Management (2.5) + Customer-centric Mindset and Agile Delivery Management (2.5)

**Семестр 4 (30 CP):**

- Master Thesis (30)

### Специализации (минимум 20 CP в одной области)

**Software Engineering (обязательно для всех, 15 CP):**

- Software Construction, Architecture and Engineering (m)
- Quality Engineering (m)
- Architectural Strategy (m)

Elective: Advances in Software Engineering, Advanced Databases.

**Cybersecurity:**

- Cryptography, System Security, Network Security (main content)
- Cybercriminology (further core)

**Artificial Intelligence:**

- Deep Learning, Intelligent Autonomous Systems, Symbolic AI (main content)
- Text Analysis & NLP, Data Analytics, Machine Learning (further core)

**Breakthrough:** Quantum Informatics (me, 5)

Все студенты обязаны набрать минимум 5 CP из AI и 5 CP из Cybersecurity. При специализации в одной из областей — все три main content модуля этой области.

### Три трека (15 CP)

| Трек | Модули |
|------|--------|
| **Startup** | Capstone Project I + II + III (по 5 CP) |
| **Industry** | Internship 10 CP + 1 technical CORE 5 CP |
| **Research** | Research Lab I + II + Research Project (по 5 CP) |

### Management (все обязательны, 15 CP)

- Agile Product Development & Design
- Product Innovation & Marketing
- Transformational Change Management

### Leadership / Academic Skills (все обязательны, 15 CP)

По 2.5 CP каждый: Entrepreneurship & Intrapreneurship, Communication for Executives, Organizational Behavior, Academic Writing / Intercultural Training, Agile Leadership and Strategic Management, Customer-centric Mindset and Agile Delivery Management.

### Intended Learning Outcomes (15 пунктов)

Критическая оценка технологий и SE-методологий; AI; cryptography и security; cross-disciplinary management; leadership; планирование и документирование research projects; научные методы; этика; teamwork; professional standards.

### Поступление

- Бакалавриат по CS / SE / IT или смежной области
- Минимум **60 ECTS** CS-related topics (условное зачисление возможно)
- Motivation letter, CV, transcripts, degree certificate
- English: TOEFL 90 / IELTS 6.5 / Duolingo 110
- Recommendation letter (optional)

### Примечание для ProfDeck

Первый SE-модуль ведёт **Prof. Dr. Bertrand Meyer** (Eiffel, Design by Contract, object-oriented programming) — пересекается с курсом OOP Peter Müller в проекте.

## 4. Сравнение программ

| | BSc (обе когорты) | MSc CSSE |
|--|-------------------|----------|
| ECTS | 180 | 120 |
| Семестров | 6 | 4 |
| Модель | 4C + CONSTRUCTOR Track | Technical CORE + Management + Leadership + Track + Thesis |
| Фокус | Широкий CS foundation | SE + специализация (SE / AI / Cybersecurity) + leadership |
| Практика | Internship 15 CP | Startup / Industry / Research track 15 CP |
| Финал | Bachelor thesis 12 + seminar 3 | Master thesis 30 |
| Management | Нет (только soft skills в CONSTRUCTOR Track) | 15 CP обязательных management-модулей |
